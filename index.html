01 · THE CONCEPT

What exactly is a digital footprint?

A digital footprint is the trail of information created by your activity online. Some actions are intentional; others are generated or collected in the background.

01

Post

A photo, comment, location or profile detail goes online.

02

Share

People can forward, repost, download or screenshot it.

03

Store

Platforms and other people may retain copies.

04

Remain

Old content can resurface long after the original post.

ACTIVE FOOTPRINT

What you deliberately leave

Posts, comments, photos, likes, profile information, reviews and form submissions are examples of information you actively provide.

PASSIVE FOOTPRINT

What happens in the background

Browsing activity, device information, cookies and approximate location can contribute to a passive footprint.

05 · PROJECT METHOD

How the study was built.

01

Survey

A 15-question questionnaire was used to understand social-media habits, security practices and digital-footprint awareness.

02

Collect

The dataset contains 38 responses. Multiple-choice questions were retained as recorded.

03

Analyze

Responses were converted into counts, percentages and key findings to identify awareness gaps.

04

Engage

The findings can be used as the evidence base for the CEP awareness activity and presentation.

Data notes

  • 78.9% of respondents are aged 18–26, so the results mainly represent young adults.
  • Answers are self-reported and should not be treated as a population-wide estimate.
  • Platform and personal-information questions allowed multiple selections, so totals can exceed 100%.
  • One respondent selected both “None of these” and other personal-information options; the response is retained as recorded.
